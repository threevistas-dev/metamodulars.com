C-Series

The classroom that replaces portables for good.

A permanent, DSA-ready classroom building from one to eight rooms. Clerestory daylight reaches the back wall, the timber ceiling stays exposed, and the whole thing is set on your site in days rather than months.

Designed, not just assembled.

Daylight to the back wall

North-facing clerestory glazing above the teaching wall, so the room is lit without glare on screens and without the lights on for most of the day.

Exposed timber ceiling

The structure is the finish. Sixteen feet at the high side, warm to look at, and quieter than a hard-lid classroom of the same size.

Built for the acoustic standard

Wall and roof assemblies detailed to meet classroom acoustic criteria, verified in the plant rather than argued about on site.

Its own entry and canopy

Covered entry, ramp, and landing designed as part of the building, not bolted on after delivery.

Systems that districts can maintain

Zoned heat pumps, standard filter sizes, and serviceable fixtures chosen for a maintenance department, not a spec sheet.

Finishes chosen with you

Exterior palette, entry color, and interior finish selected from a developed range so the building belongs to your campus.

Four layouts to start from.

Each one is a settled plan we adapt to your site and program. Drawn to the same scale.
